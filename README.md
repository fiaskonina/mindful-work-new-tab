## What is this?

**fiaskonina — Mindful Work & AI New Tab** replaces the default browser new tab page with an intentional micro-coaching check-in. It invites you to pause, reflect, and recalibrate your workday with gentle, solution-focused questions rooted in ICF-certified business coaching frameworks.

### Select your current state:
* 🌪️ **Overwhelmed or Rushed** - Ground yourself, regain agency, and filter out noise.
* 🧱 **Stuck or Uncertain** - Break analysis paralysis with a micro-step.
* 🚀 **Inspired or Big Project Delivered** - Celebrate and anchor your progress.
* 🌿 **Low Energy or Contemplative** - Practice self-compassion and gentle pacing.
* ⚖️ **At a Crossroads** - Align with your core values and make conscious choices.
* 🎯 **Calm & Seeking Clarity** - Protect your focus and work on what truly matters.

---

## Key Principles

- **100% Private & Offline:** Runs entirely in your browser using local storage. Zero analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and no network calls.
- **Bilingual:** Instant toggle between Finnish (FI) and English (EN).
- **Calm Aesthetic:** Designed with soft tones (#FDF5E6) and quiet typography (Lora & Sora) to reduce sensory overload.
